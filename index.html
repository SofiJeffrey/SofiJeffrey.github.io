05 / A little about me

It started with
a model of beer.

I started out wanting to practice mathematical modeling. I just so happened to be modeling beer.

For three years, I mostly worked on the model and only brewed once. That work led me to Anheuser-Busch, where I fell in love with fermentation and beverages. At Dogfish Head, I found another interest: sensory science.

Much of my work has been with nonalcoholic and low-alcohol products, including energy drinks. I love how tangible it is: taking an abstract mathematical idea, calibrating it against experimental data, and using it to make something beautiful and delicious.

Away from the lab, I enjoy camping and backpacking, poetry, creative writing, and French language and culture.
